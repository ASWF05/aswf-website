Responsible Communication

Information should be clear, verified and respectful.

ASWF aims to communicate its work responsibly. Updates should represent genuine activities and avoid unsupported claims, exaggerated figures or misleading information.

Verified Information

Programme information and organisational announcements should be confirmed before publication.

Respect for Dignity

Stories and images involving communities should be shared thoughtfully and respectfully.

Clear Updates

Important information should be presented in a simple, understandable and responsible way.

Our Approach

No invented stories. No inflated numbers. Just responsible communication.
