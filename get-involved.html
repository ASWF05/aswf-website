Get Involved

Your time, skills and voice can matter.

Meaningful community work is built through people who are willing to contribute. Explore ways to support the mission of Ayesha Social Welfare Foundation according to your interests, skills and capacity.

01 — Ways to Contribute

Choose your way to make a difference.

You do not need to contribute in just one way. Depending on your experience and availability, you can support ASWF through time, expertise, connections or community participation.

01

Volunteer

Support programmes, awareness activities, community outreach, events and other practical initiatives.

02

Share Your Skills

Contribute professional, creative, digital, educational, communication or organisational skills.

03

Partner

Organisations and institutions can explore meaningful collaborations aligned with community needs.

04

Support

Provide responsible financial or resource support for community-focused activities.

05

Advocacy

Help spread awareness about education, rights, dignity, equality and social responsibility.

06

Community Participation

Connect ASWF with communities, institutions and people who may benefit from or contribute to responsible initiatives.

02 — Volunteer Opportunities

Where your contribution can fit.

Opportunities can vary according to programme requirements, location, availability and the skills needed at a particular time.

ED

Education

Support learning, educational awareness and community-based education initiatives.

CO

Community Outreach

Help with local engagement, awareness activities and community-level coordination.

DM

Digital & Media

Contribute to content, social media, design, photography, communications or digital work.

PS

Professional Skills

Offer expertise in areas such as administration, technology, finance, management or communications.

WE

Women Empowerment

Support awareness, participation and initiatives focused on dignity, opportunity and empowerment.

LA

Legal Awareness

Support responsible awareness activities related to rights, legal literacy and access to information.

YS

Youth & Sports

Participate in youth engagement, sports and community development activities.

EA

Events & Activities

Assist with planning, coordination and practical support for community activities and events.

03 — Getting Started

A simple way to begin.

We encourage people to start with a clear conversation about their interests, skills and availability before deciding how they can contribute.

STEP 01

Reach Out

Contact ASWF and briefly tell us about yourself and how you would like to contribute.

STEP 02

Understand

Discuss your interests, skills, location and availability with the appropriate team.

STEP 03

Explore

Identify a suitable area of participation based on current organisational needs.

STEP 04

Contribute

Begin contributing responsibly while staying connected with the relevant team or activity.
