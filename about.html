01 — Introduction

Who We Are

Building stronger communities through meaningful social action, awareness and empowerment.

Ayesha Social Welfare Foundation (ASWF) is a student-led social welfare organisation established with the objective of contributing towards an educated, empowered, healthy and just society.

The Foundation works at the community level through initiatives in education, social welfare, legal awareness, healthcare, women empowerment, youth development, community development and other areas of public welfare.

ASWF seeks to encourage young people and community members to participate in meaningful social action and to create greater awareness of rights, opportunities and responsibilities.

The Foundation believes in transforming social challenges into opportunities for empowerment through education, awareness, participation and collective action.

02 — Direction

Mission & Vision

Our work is guided by a long-term vision and a practical grassroots mission.

Our Vision

An inclusive and empowered society.

To build an inclusive and empowered society where every individual can live with dignity and has access to education, justice, healthcare, equal opportunities and social development.

Our Mission

Turning awareness into responsible action.

To work at the grassroots level for the welfare and empowerment of communities by promoting education, legal awareness, healthcare, women empowerment, youth participation and community development, while encouraging responsible citizenship and collective social action.

03 — Objectives

What We Aim To Do

ASWF's objectives provide a broad framework for its charitable, educational and social welfare activities.

01

To promote education and educational opportunities among students and underserved communities.

02

To create awareness regarding legal rights, duties and access to justice.

03

To promote healthcare awareness and facilitate community-oriented health initiatives.

04

To work towards the empowerment, education, safety and development of women and girls.

05

To encourage youth participation in social welfare and community development.

06

To support marginalised, vulnerable and underserved sections of society.

07

To organise awareness campaigns, seminars, workshops, camps and community programmes.

08

To promote social equality, human dignity, justice and harmony.

09

To encourage environmental awareness and responsible community participation.

10

To promote sports, leadership and personality development among young people.

11

To collaborate with professionals, institutions, NGOs, government bodies and other stakeholders for social welfare initiatives.

12

To undertake such other lawful charitable and social welfare activities as are consistent with the objectives of the Foundation.
