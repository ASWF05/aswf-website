03 — Our Approach

Every photograph should respect the story behind it.

Community work is about people, not simply photographs. When visual stories are shared, they should reflect the dignity, context and reality of the people and communities involved.

ASWF aims to build its visual archive responsibly, using authentic material and clear descriptions rather than creating an impression of activities that have not been documented.
