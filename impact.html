Impact across key areas

Our programmes combine direct assistance, awareness, student development and community participation.

01

Healthcare

Free medical and eye-care camps have provided check-ups, specialist consultations and medicines to community members.

Documented: 506 beneficiaries across three recorded health camps.
02

Education

ASWF works with students and communities through literacy activities, educational camps, competitions and encouragement towards education.

Activities: literacy drive, quiz, essay, drawing, calligraphy and other educational programmes.
03

Career Guidance

Students have been supported through career counselling and guidance around competitive examinations and future educational pathways.

Reporting approach: no participant number is claimed here until an exact recorded count is available.
04

Legal Awareness

Legal-awareness initiatives connect communities with information about rights, responsibilities, justice and emerging concerns such as cyber crime.

Patwari Nagla: 100+ people interacted, supported by 120 law-student volunteers and 4 advocates.
05

Environment

ASWF encourages responsible community participation through environmental awareness and plantation activities.

Documented: 100+ trees planted during the Independence Day drive on 15 August 2024.
06

Women & Youth

Women empowerment, youth participation, sports and leadership development form part of ASWF's wider social-welfare objectives.

Approach: awareness, participation, leadership and community-based engagement.
Programme evidence

Selected documented activities

Specific dates, locations and figures are shown where they have been recorded by the Foundation.

Healthcare that reached communities

ASWF's documented health activities include free check-ups, specialist consultations and medicines for community members.

•22 August 2024: Free Medical Camp — 118 people received free check-up and medicines.
•21 January 2025: Free Eye Check-up & Medicine Camp — 132 beneficiaries.
•12–13 July 2026: Free Medical Camp — 256 beneficiaries with multiple specialist services and free medicines.
Total documented beneficiaries across these three camps: 506.

Education beyond the classroom

ASWF's education work is designed to encourage learning, confidence, awareness and informed choices among students.

•Rural literacy engagement with families and children at Bangra Bansidhar village, Muzaffarpur, Bihar.
•Educational camps featuring essay writing, quizzes, drawing and calligraphy competitions.
•Career counselling and guidance relating to competitive examinations and future educational pathways.
•Summer Camp activities including quiz, essay and debate competitions.
Where an exact student count has not been formally recorded here, ASWF does not publish an invented figure.
